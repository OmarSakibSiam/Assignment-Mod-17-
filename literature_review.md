
## 1st paper : An Exploration of Clustering Algorithms for Customer Segmentation in the UK Retail Market

=> John et al. (2023) performed customer segmentation on UCI online retail dataset. The research was based on customers purchasing behavior. The authors compared five different clustering algorithms which are  K-Means, Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM), and DBSCAN, and evaluated their performance using the Silhouette Score.


## 2nd paper: A Framework for Customer Segmentation to Improve Marketing Strategies Using Machine Learning

=> The segmentation by  Ashraf et al. (2025) was done by using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM). This research was done on four clustering techniques, namely, K-Means, Mini Batch K-Means, Spectral Clustering, and Fuzzy K-Means, employing the Silhouette Score. Among all the models K-Means performed best. Which achieved a  Silhouette Score of 0.432619.


## 3rd paper: Customer Segmentation by Using RFM Model and Clustering Methods: A Case Study in Retail Industry (2018)

=> Dogan et al. (2018) states in their research that the RFM model was used in grouping of the retail clients in groups of 700,032 clients based on their buying behaviors. The study employed two clustering techniques where the customers were clustered on the basis of their recency, frequency, and monetary values. It was found out that customers cannot be segmented on the basis of their spending alone.
